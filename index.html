About TrustPath AI

1 in 7 job postings online is fraudulent. TrustPath AI independently verifies company authenticity, recruiter identity, and hiring-process integrity so job seekers can apply with confidence.

Problem Catalogue

There are several ways fraudulent job opportunities operate. Each is mapped to a specific AI solution.

PS-01

Fake Company Creation

Fraudsters create fictitious companies with fabricated websites, addresses, employee profiles, and job openings.

AI solution: Company existence verification, domain intelligence, business identity checks, and independent source corroboration.
PS-02

Genuine Company Impersonation

Scammers pretend to represent Google, Amazon, Microsoft, startups, or other legitimate employers.

AI solution: Compare official domains, recruiter identities, company contact details, and career-page job listings.
PS-03

Lookalike Websites & Domains

Fraudsters use domains resembling genuine company sites (e.g. careers-stripe.com instead of stripe.com).

AI solution: Domain similarity scoring, WHOIS age checks, SSL validation, and typosquatting detection.
PS-04

Payment & Deposit Scams

Candidates are asked to pay for training materials, background checks, or equipment deposits before starting.

AI solution: NLP scanning for payment-related language, financial red-flag patterns, and urgency triggers.
PS-05

Off-Platform Communication

Recruiters move conversations to WhatsApp, Telegram, or personal emails to avoid platform moderation.

AI solution: Communication channel analysis and cross-platform recruiter identity matching.
PS-06

Overpayment & Data Theft

Fake "employers" send overpayment checks or harvest Aadhaar/PAN/SSN during fake onboarding flows.

AI solution: Hiring-process sequence analysis and sensitive-data request detection at abnormal stages.
